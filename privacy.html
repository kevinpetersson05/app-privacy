Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This is a private, self-hosted scheduling tool. It is used only by its owner, to publish content to accounts the owner controls.

What data it uses

When an account is connected, the tool stores the access token that the platform (for example Google/YouTube or Meta/Instagram) issues, plus the account's name and picture. It uses them only to upload and schedule the owner's own posts and to show basic post statistics.

Where it is kept

All data stays on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in the platform's own settings (Google Account > Security > Third-party apps, or Instagram > Settings > Apps and websites). Removing a channel in the tool deletes its stored token.

Google user data is handled in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
